04 Typography

Bauhaus 93

Bauhaus 93 is the display face: its bubble-round letterforms are built for short, large headlines. Keep it for titles, not for body copy, buttons or long-form text.

TTF
AÉM
Association Étudiante de Mila

Alegreya

Alegreya is the working typeface for body copy, buttons and labels across web and print. Its warm, humanist serif forms soften the geometry of Bauhaus.

06 Misuse

Don't recolor the mark outside the approved palette.

Don't rotate or tilt the logo.

Don't stretch or distort the proportions.

Don't add shadows, glows or bevels.

Don't place a color version on a low-contrast background.

Don't reduce the opacity.
